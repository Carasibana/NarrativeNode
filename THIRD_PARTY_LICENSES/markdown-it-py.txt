markdown-it-py
https://github.com/executablebooks/markdown-it-py
License: MIT

Used by the backend export renderers to convert markdown a writer types into
free-form text fields (entity / relationship / knowledge descriptions,
text-type attribute values, notes, circumstance / motivator / perspective
bodies, scene descriptions) into HTML, which is then rendered per export
format. Bundles its sole dependency, mdurl (MIT), whose licence follows.
